SKILLY — Module 01: Public Website

1. Module Overview

Module Name: Public Website

Purpose:
The Public Website is the public-facing entry point of SKILLY. It introduces the platform, explains its purpose and major capabilities, and guides visitors toward registration and login.

This module is accessible without authentication.
