# SKILLY — Module 04: College / TPO

## 1. Module Overview

**Module Name:** College / TPO

**Purpose:**  
The College / TPO module provides a centralized platform for colleges and Training & Placement Officers to manage student, training, internship and placement-related activities and coordinate with industry.

## 10. Eligibility & Shortlisting

The TPO can identify students who meet the requirements of an internship or placement opportunity.

### High-Level Process

```text
Company Requirements
        ↓
Student Data
        ↓
Eligibility Check
        ↓
Skill / Requirement Matching
        ↓
Eligible Students
        ↓
Shortlist
```

This supports faster coordination between companies and eligible students.

- Alumni / mentor personal dashboard

- Detailed AI model implementation
- Detailed database schema
